# Test Notes

## Test Setup

- **Learning question:** Does turning the whole pile into drafts in one photo
  session get a person from "pile" to "drafts ready" without wanting to stop or
  put it off?
- **Secondary question:** Do they believe the drafts are good enough to finish
  and post?
- **Prototype:** [prototype/index.html](prototype/index.html), v2 with
  multi-select platforms and Wizard of Oz recognition.
- **Simulated parts:** item recognition (the facilitator types the names); no
  real posting.
- **Task:** "Imagine it's Saturday afternoon and you have a pile of clothes
  you've been meaning to sell. Show me how you'd use this to get them ready to
  sell."
- **Facilitator rule:** only ask "What would you do next?" during the task.
